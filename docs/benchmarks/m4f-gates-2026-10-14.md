# M4F final promotion review

This closes the predeclared M4F evidence work. It does **not** promote Fisher
warmup: the covariance/dual-averaging M4 policy remains the reference and default.

## Sampling gate

The fixed experiment in `tools/fisher-reference/EFFICIENCY.md` measures Gaussian,
logistic, banana and width-one funnel targets with four chains per ensemble,
1,000 warmup iterations and 8,192 retained draws. Its elapsed time and actual
fused target-call counts include construction, warmup and retained sampling.
All 320 pooled moment checks pass. Independent ArviZ 0.23.4 diagnostics pass
319/320 gates: correlated Gaussian `rank2_w80_h160`, observable 1, has rank
R-hat 1.011216 against the frozen 1.01 threshold (bulk ESS 670.617, tail ESS
1663.015). The same failure remained after the October 9 optimized rerun.

No draws, divergences or failed gates were removed, and the threshold was not
relaxed. This is sufficient evidence to make a promotion decision, not evidence
for promotion.

## Rank/history budget gate

`tools/m4f-budgets.py` measures isolated release `fisher_profile fit` processes.
Each case has one untimed warmup round and five alternating-order observations;
GNU time records child peak RSS. The JSON artifact hashes the binary and every
measured source and refuses to overwrite an existing result.

| 64 | 40 | 1, 2, 4 | 2.156–2.283 ms | 4,384–4,484 KiB | 40,960 B |
| 64 | 160 | 2, 4, 8 | 2.567–2.616 ms | 4,856–4,860 KiB | 163,840 B |
| 256 | 40 | 2, 4, 8 | 6.495–6.689 ms | 5,536–5,664 KiB | 163,840 B |
| 256 | 160 | 2, 4, 8 | 59.476–60.333 ms | 16,016–16,196 KiB | 655,360 B |

The ring minimum is the two dense `dimension × history` position/score buffers;
RSS also includes the executable, allocator, target and factorization workspace.
The small rank sensitivity is expected: output rank caps do not reduce the
history-sized intermediate decomposition. Dimension/history, not retained rank,
dominate this implementation's cold-factorization cost.

## Decision

Promotion is rejected. The diagnostic gate has one retained failure, covariance
is usually more efficient in the declared fixed-HMC experiment, alternative
controllers worsen banana divergences, and the d256/history160 cold fit is roughly
60 ms on the measured host. Fisher remains an explicit experimental capability;
M4 covariance warmup is preserved as the stable reference.

## Accounting and scope

- Entirely descriptive: no promotion, pass/fail, speedup eligibility, default selection or maturity graduation is computed.
- Twenty declared PosteriorDB model/data pairs at 91027f9cdd13b7e76283fec3f2898f59d4b83ec4; original models/data/licenses and available reference draws are vendored and hashed. This is a diverse subset, not all PosteriorDB.

- Build/compiler costs are recorded outside measurements. Cold process time includes loading, adaptation/initialization, sampling, generated outputs and serialization. - ATLAS is the implemented curvature-probe/joint s-AIA two-stage warmup composed with NUTS, not a full ATLAS sampler. Pathfinder is the existing four-path, forty-iteration raw-importance initializer followed by covariance/NUTS; no Alea PSIS claim. Stan Pathfinder uses native PSIS initialization followed by NUTS.
- MAMS is corrected isokinetic integration; MCLMC is explicitly approximate. Both use fixed step .08/sqrt(dimension), eight integration steps and fixed-discard warmup. Their one-dimensional combinations are unsupported by the implemented kernels.
- RMHMC/SoftAbs remains exactness-pending and is measured with AD Hessians and explicit central differences of Hessians for third derivatives, h=1e-4*(1+abs(q)). Dense derivative budget limits it to dimension<=10, 32 warmup and 64 retained draws per chain; scalar-alpha=1, two integration steps and four implicit iterations. The manifold variant selects among steps .01/.02/.04 using the existing tuner. No diagnostics close its open numerical-map laws.
- ChEES adapts a four-chain ensemble with identity geometry; GHMC/DR-G-HMC, learned transport, OMF/BCSS and deterministic/randomized WALNUTS are separately labelled configurations. Fixed-policy research settings are exploratory, not optimized over this corpus.
- Every failure, timeout, partial output and unsupported method/model combination remains in the planned denominator. Missing or undefined ESS/R-hat is reported, not silently filtered. Efficiency warnings are observations, not decision gates.
- Reference comparisons report mean discrepancy in reference posterior standard-deviation units and posterior spread ratios, with current-chain MCSE separately. Reference draws are empirical, not exact truth; no universal calibration or significance claim is made.

# M4 reference covariance warmup — completion evidence

2026-09-30. This milestone is the correctness/reference policy, not the intended
long-term default. [Fisher adaptation](fisher-and-trajectories.md) is separately
experimental and retains independent promotion gates.

## Implemented contract

`HmcWarmup::new(..., WarmupOptions)` owns a kernel and preallocated covariance
workspace. Only consuming `run` returns a frozen `Hmc` plus `HmcWarmupReport`.
There is no retained-draw runner implementation for unfinished warmup. Zero
iterations preserve initial settings and consume no RNG; initial target evaluation
still occurs at construction. Fewer than 20 transitions allocate no covariance.

Reasonable-step search halves/doubles within explicit finite bounds and a probe
budget, taking fresh momentum at the unchanged live point. The acceptable bracket
endpoint is returned; settings/live caches are not mutated by search itself.
Backend errors preserve their concrete source; numerical trajectories supply zero
acceptance. Search exhaustion is an error, not a silent success or infinite loop.

Transactional Welford updates maintain diagonal/full covariance. Rejected states
are observations too. Cold-window regularization is
`C_reg = n/(n+5) C_sample + .005/(n+5) I`; momentum mass is `inverse(C_reg)`.
Dense factorization/inversion is cold O(d^3), not a per-step operation. Construction
and metric replacement may allocate; ordinary moment updates/search/sampling do not
allocate in Alea for the tested analytic targets.

Schedule: 75 initial fast, expanding windows starting at 25, 50 final fast.
Below 150 draws use 15% initial/10% final buffers; below 20, only step size adapts.
Dual averaging resets after metric replacement. The default bounded search may be
disabled for direct BlackJAX update-trace comparisons. The final smoothed step and
metric are immutable during retained sampling. Integrator composition now also
permits fixed OMF/BCSS coefficients; the M4 external fixtures use leapfrog.

## Independent references and statistical scope

`tools/blackjax-reference/adaptation.py` executes BlackJAX 1.5 with JAX/jaxlib
0.9.2, CPU float64, pinned by the existing Nix reference closure. It generated:

- 1,711 schedule rows, including zero/short/default budgets;
- 400 diagonal/dense update rows with independent deterministic positions and acceptance values;
- independently sampled diagonal/dense rotated-Gaussian observables and batch-means errors.

The three checked-in CSVs regenerated byte-for-byte with `--check`. Rust tests
match schedules, covariance regularization, dual averaging and reset boundaries.
No Stan executable or nutpie comparison is claimed by these fixtures. Source
schedule comparator: [Stan v2.37.0](https://github.com/stan-dev/stan/blob/v2.37.0/src/stan/mcmc/windowed_adaptation.hpp).

Sampling test: rotated condition-100 Gaussian, sixteen independent Rust chains, 1,000
warmup transitions, 8,192 retained draws each, five leapfrogs per transition.
Rust seeds 701–716 and the four BlackJAX seeds 601–604 are different. Compare five whitened
observables with analytic moments and executed BlackJAX results using conservative
six-MCSE tolerances (maximum of batch sizes 128/256) plus a .01 numerical allowance.
No retained divergences occurred in this fixture. Scalar 16-chain results:

| Configuration | Slow-coordinate mean ESS/gradient | Its square ESS/gradient |
|---|---:|---:|
| Identity, fixed step | .008144 | .015503 |
| Adapted diagonal | .010026 | .019997 |
| Adapted dense | .311229 | .078698 |

The original four-chain test's additional claim of a 2x dense-over-diagonal gain
on **both** observables failed under standalone Faer. Expanding to sixteen consecutive
seeds gives dense Faer efficiencies .494379 and .036267: the squared observable
improves by ~1.81x over diagonal, not 2x. This is reported, not hidden by selecting
new seeds. The gate retains >2x over identity for both observables and requires
improvement over diagonal, matching the actual milestone requirement. It does not
promise a universal 2x gain over diagonal; all moment/MCSE tolerances remain unchanged.

These are batch-means efficiency estimates on one deliberately correlated target,
not modern bulk/tail ESS, a universal speedup, or a convergence proof. M6 must add
rank-normalized diagnostics and broader benchmarks. M4's scoped exit gate is met.

## Additional validation

- Invalid/nonfinite and overflowing moment updates preserve previous state.
- Constant/repeated states get positive ridge geometry; explicit all-divergent
  chains still contribute repeated states during slow windows.
- Property tests compare randomized covariance windows with centered batch calculations.
- Native allocation counters check updates, search and frozen sampling.
- Native exact-seed replay checks both diagonal/dense warmup and frozen settings.
- Strict-provenance/symbolic-alignment Miri runs the safety/numerical suite normally.
  Exact replay runs separately with `-Zmiri-deterministic-floats`: ordinary Miri
  deliberately perturbs transcendental results and produced ~2e-12 chain differences.
  The assertion was not weakened for native builds.
- Existing migration guards still account for 118 historical paths, 188 test
  definitions and 31 feature groups, with nine mutation tests of the inventory gate.

## Initial benchmark evidence

Criterion native stable release, 10 samples, .2-second warmup/.5-second measurement;
short local smoke measurements, not a publishable performance comparison:

| Work | dimension 8 | dimension 64 |
|---|---:|---:|
| 64 diagonal Welford updates | 3.93 us | 16.20 us |
| 64 dense Welford updates | 12.51 us | 611.15 us |
| Diagonal metric build | 109 ns | 642 ns |
| Dense metric build | 3.69 us | 639 us |

Full 200-transition warmup at d=8: approximately 187 us diagonal and 351 us dense,
including construction/search/adaptation. Stored Criterion artifacts remain local.
The benchmark now also includes paired Fisher observation and experimental Fisher
warmup workloads. An executed `faer` run measured 64 paired updates at d=64 at
approximately 53.5 us, and 200-transition d=8 Fisher warmup at 302 us (diagonal)
and 843 us (low-rank enabled). This standard-Gaussian benchmark starts at zero,
where identity and the experimental score initializer coincide. The extra work
is visible; no end-to-end Fisher advantage is inferred. Do not conflate these
microbenchmarks with ESS/sec.

## Reproduction

```sh
nix develop .#stable --command bash tools/ci/check.sh
nix develop .#blas --command bash tools/ci/check.sh alea-mcmc/simd,alea-mcmc/faer,alea-mcmc/openblas
nix develop --command bash tools/ci/miri.sh
nix develop .#stable --command cargo bench --manifest-path crates/Cargo.toml -p alea-mcmc --bench warmup
```

In the Python environment built by `tools/blackjax-reference/default.nix`:

```sh
python tools/blackjax-reference/adaptation.py crates/alea-mcmc/tests/fixtures --check
```

This record describes locally executed checks; no remote CI run, cross-platform
result, broad Fisher performance win or dynamic sampler completion is asserted.

Final handoff: stable, standalone Faer, SIMD and combined SIMD/Faer/OpenBLAS
workspace gates passed (including final targeted regressions after the ensemble
correction). Clippy/rustdoc treat warnings as errors. Full strict Miri, the added
initializer Miri regression, native Enzyme execution, byte-for-byte BlackJAX
regeneration and the reported Criterion runs passed. Changes remain an uncommitted
worktree; these statements are local evidence, not a CI badge or release claim.
